The job of each block

#BlockIts job
1Logo and trust stripLogos, counts or certifications, so the reader knows they are in the right place.
2Headline and subheadThe promise, then the mechanism in one line.
3A 60 to 90 second videoMuted and captioned. It makes the promise credible.
4Three proof pointsNumbers, not adjectives. Leave it empty rather than invent one.
5The formFirst name, last name, work email. Add a field only if you will use it.
6Your method, namedIn 3 to 5 steps: why your way works when theirs didn't.
7TestimonialsEach one answers one objection.
8An FAQYour objection list, answered.
9A sticky buttonFollows the reader down the page, so the sign-up stays one tap away.

The above-the-fold check

Open your page on a 1366 x 768 laptop screen and don't scroll.

  • The form is on the first screen, without scrolling.
  • The form appears again lower down the page.
  • No navigation link leads off the page.
  • The page reads well at 375 px (phone), 768 px (tablet) and 1280 px (laptop).
  • There is a consent line under the form, with links to the privacy policy and terms.
  • A sign-up from my phone shows up in my CRM with its source.

Two tests for every block

Test 1

Does it answer an objection or drive one action?

Test 2

Does it bring the first result earlier?

A block that fails both comes off the page.

The prompt that drafts your page

Write my landing page
Write the copy for my [FILL: registration / seat / video / booking / join] page, block by block:
1. Trust strip line. 2. Headline (the promise, under 12 words) and subhead (the mechanism in one line); give 2 headline options, one benefit-led and one problem-led. 3. A 60 to 90 second video script (muted, captioned) that makes the promise credible. 4. Three proof points as numbers, only from my proof list. 5. Form heading and button label. 6. My method in 3 to 5 named steps, one line each. 7. Where testimonials go and the objection each must answer. 8. An FAQ: 6 questions from my buyer's objections, answered in 2 to 3 sentences. 9. Sticky button label.
Event line: [FILL: or "evergreen"]. Use my Funnel Brief. Never invent proof: leave [FILL: proof] where I have none.

Test two headlines, not two pages

One benefit-led, one problem-led. Never test a second, thinner page.

The mistake to avoid

Asking for a phone number you will never call. Every extra field costs sign-ups.
