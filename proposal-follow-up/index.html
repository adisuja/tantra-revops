The 7-Day Proposal Follow-Up

Most proposals don't get a no. They get silence. Send the proposal within one business day with what's not included written down, then three short messages in seven days. After that a person takes over, and every deal ends with a decision or a reason.

The timeline

WhenChannelWhat goes out
Day 0Email + WhatsAppThe proposal, within one business day of the call, with the link to book the review call inside it
Day 1WhatsAppA short note: did it arrive, and is a review call worth booking
Day 3EmailOne piece of proof from a real client, close to their situation
Day 7WhatsApp"Should I keep your proposal open, or is now not the right time? Either answer is fine."
Day 8Your CRMNo booking and no decision: the deal moves to Follow-Up
Follow-UpCall or voice noteTwice a week for two weeks, by a person, not an automation
After 2 weeksYour CRMStill no decision after two weeks in Follow-Up: the deal moves to nurture

A reply stops the chasing

Any reply ends the sequence and a person takes over. "STOP" on any channel ends every message for good. WhatsApp goes only between 8am and 8pm in their time zone.

The three messages

Day 1 · WhatsApp
Hi {first_name}, the proposal is in your inbox. If it's worth a walk-through, the review call link is inside. [One line on the one stage you agreed to fix first]
Day 3 · Email
Subject: [Their goal] Hi {first_name}, one example close to yours: [a real client, where they started, what was built, what changed, with the period]. The review call link is in your proposal.
Day 7 · WhatsApp
Hi {first_name}, should I keep your proposal open, or is now not the right time? Either answer is fine. If you want to go through it, book here: {review_call_link}

Never

  • Invent a deadline, a discount or a guarantee to get a yes.
  • Send a proposal without the review call link inside it.
  • Leave a deal in Proposal Sent with no date on the next step.
