The Offer Ladder Worksheet

Almost nobody buys the expensive thing first. Lay out five rungs from the first time someone notices you to your biggest offer, and price the first paid step so it pays for the traffic that brought the buyer in.

The five rungs

RungExample: a B2B software companyYour offerPriceThe step to the next rung
AttentionFree courses and a free tool
LeadA webinar seat or a free trial
Entry offerA starter plan
Core offerA professional plan
FlagshipAn enterprise plan with onboarding

The rung that carries the ladder

The entry offer. Price it so the revenue it brings in covers the cost of the traffic that produced it, and your top of funnel pays for itself.

The entry price check

Cost of traffic per buyer
≤
Entry offer price

If the cost is higher than the price, change one of three things: the price, what the entry offer includes, or the channel that brings the traffic. Use your own numbers. If you don't know the cost yet, write "unknown, will measure" and measure it on the next run.

How to use it

1

Fill the rungs you already have

Use your real offers and prices. A rung you don't have yet is marked TO BUILD, not invented.

2

Run the entry price check

This is the one number that decides whether more traffic makes you money or costs you.

3

Name the step up from each rung

What moves a lead to the entry offer, and an entry buyer to the core offer. One step each.

The prompt that builds your ladder

Build my offer ladder
YOUR TASK
Build my offer ladder: five rungs from attention to my biggest offer.

THE FACTS TO USE
What I sell today, with prices: [FILL]
What it costs me in traffic to win one buyer of my first paid offer: [FILL, or "unknown, will measure"]

RULES
- Five rungs in order: Attention, Lead, Entry offer, Core offer, Flagship.
- The entry offer must bring in at least what it cost to win that buyer. If it doesn't, say so and suggest a price or a change.
- Each rung leads to the one above it. Name the step that moves a buyer up.
- Only my real offers and prices. A rung I don't have yet is marked TO BUILD.

WHAT TO RETURN
A table: rung, the offer, the price, the step to the next rung. Then the entry price check in two lines.

CHECK BEFORE YOU ANSWER
Every price is one I gave you.
