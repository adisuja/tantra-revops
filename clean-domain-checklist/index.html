The Clean Domain Sending Checklist

Your invoices, contracts and customer replies leave from your main domain. Cold outreach should never leave from the same one. Tick the boxes, then send.

1. The domains

  • Buy two or three domains that look like your brand, such as getyourbrand.com and tryyourbrand.com.
  • Point each one at your main website, so a curious prospect lands somewhere real.
  • Create two or three mailboxes per domain, in real names from your team.
  • Never send cold outreach from your main domain.

2. The four DNS records

  • MX, from your mailbox provider. One set per domain, never two.
  • SPF. For Google: v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all
  • DKIM, generated in your mailbox admin.
  • DMARC: v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:you@yourdomain.com

3. The two-week warm-up

  • Connect every mailbox to a warm-up tool and leave it running.
  • Write down the date warm-up started.
  • Wait two weeks before the first cold send.
  • Every mailbox passes SPF, DKIM and DMARC on a test email.

4. The sending rules

  • Send between 8am and 8pm, their time.
  • Every email has a one-click yes and a one-click no.
  • A No is final. That person never hears from you again.
  • Start with a small batch and watch the bounces. If they climb above a few percent, stop and clean the list.
