The Sales Call System

Everything after someone books a call, in order: a page that shows your proof, a person confirming within 5 minutes, a first call that finds the leak, a proposal within a day and a follow-up that ends in a decision.

The path

StepWhat happensThe rule
1 BookThe book-a-call page: calendar, questions, case studies, proofA "not yet" on budget never blocks the booking
2 ConfirmA person calls or messages to confirmWithin 5 minutes, between 08:00 and 20:00 their time
3 Call 1Find the stage that leaks and what fixing it is worthPromise honesty; answer only the objection raised
4 ProposalTheir words, the one stage to fix, what's not includedWithin one business day, with the review call link
5 Call 2Walk the proposal section by section and decideBring the person who signs off
6 Follow-upDay 1 WhatsApp, day 3 proof email, day 7 one questionThen a person takes over; every lost deal gets a reason

Call 1, in order

1

Open with honesty

"If this isn't a fit, I'll tell you straight."

2

Walk five stages

Traffic, capture, show-up, conversion, follow-up. Stop where the drop is worst.

3

Put a number on it

"If that one stage worked twice as well, what would that be worth to you a month?"

4

Answer only the objection raised

Then ask one question back.

YOUR TASK
Write the confirmation call script and the WhatsApp fallback for a booked sales call.

THE FACTS TO USE
The call: [FILL: length, video link, who runs it]
What they should bring: [FILL]

RULES
- The call happens within 5 minutes of the booking, by a person, between 08:00 and 20:00 their time.
- Under 60 seconds. Confirm the time, ask them to bring whoever signs off, say how to move it.
- If they don't pick up: one short WhatsApp with the same three points.

WHAT TO RETURN
The call script, then the WhatsApp text.

CHECK BEFORE YOU ANSWER
No pitch, no pressure, no promise of a result.

The day 7 message

Day 7 · WhatsApp
Hi {first_name}, should I keep your proposal open, or is now not the right time? Either answer is fine. If you want to go through it, book here: {review_call_link}

Never

  • Invent a deadline, a discount or a guarantee.
  • Run the decision call without the person who signs off.
  • Leave a booked call unconfirmed for hours.
