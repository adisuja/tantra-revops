The AI Webinar Funnel System

The whole webinar funnel, from the first touch to the booked call, in six stages. Each stage has one job, one thing to build and one number to watch. AI drafts every piece from one brief; a person approves.

The six stages

StageWhat you buildThe number to watch
1 TrafficCold email, LinkedIn and calendar invites to one listVisit rate
2 LandingA registration page in nine blocks, the form above the fold on a laptopSign-up rate
3 NurtureReminders on every channel, timed from the sessionShow-up rate
4 ConversionThe session in order: promise, problem, method and proof, invitation, Q&ABooking rate
5 RevenueThe booking page, the call and the proposalClose rate
6 Long-term nurtureA teaching email every week for everyone who didn't buyRebooking rate
The equation
Customers = sign-ups × show-up rate × booking rate × close rate. Fix the weakest stage only.

The AI Webinar Funnel System

Three prompts to start

Write my registration page
Write the copy for my [FILL: registration / seat / video / booking / join] page, block by block:
1. Trust strip line. 2. Headline (the promise, under 12 words) and subhead (the mechanism in one line); give 2 headline options, one benefit-led and one problem-led. 3. A 60 to 90 second video script (muted, captioned) that makes the promise credible. 4. Three proof points as numbers, only from my proof list. 5. Form heading and button label. 6. My method in 3 to 5 named steps, one line each. 7. Where testimonials go and the objection each must answer. 8. An FAQ: 6 questions from my buyer's objections, answered in 2 to 3 sentences. 9. Sticky button label.
Event line: [FILL: or "evergreen"]. Use my Funnel Brief. Never invent proof: leave [FILL: proof] where I have none.
Write my reminder sequence
Write the reminder sequence for [FILL: my event line].
Touches: 7 days before (value primer, under 200 words), 24 hours before at 08:00 their time (what to prepare, 180 to 220 words), 1 hour before (join link, 80 to 100 words), 15 minutes before (under 50 words), and live now (50 to 70 words).
For each: email subject, preheader and body, plus a WhatsApp version under 300 characters for the 24-hour, 1-hour and live touches, written as a plain statement of fact. One link per message: {join_link}. Use {first_name} and {event_time}.
Use my Funnel Brief. No hype, no invented numbers.
Write my attendee and no-show follow-ups
Write two follow-up sequences after my [FILL: session].
Attended: straight after (offer and {booking_link} or {checkout_link}), +1 day (recap plus this objection answered: "[FILL: objection 1]"), +2 days (proof plus "[FILL: objection 2]"), +3 days (last call, deadline [FILL]).
No-show: +24 hours (replay {replay_link}, closes [FILL: date]), +2 days (the one idea they missed: [FILL: idea and minute]), +5 days (replay closes tonight), +7 days (next session [FILL: date]).
Subject, preheader and body under 150 words each, one link each, plus WhatsApp versions under 300 characters for the first message of each branch.
Use my Funnel Brief. Proof only from my proof list.
