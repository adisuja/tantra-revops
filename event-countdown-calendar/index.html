The rules

1

Date every post first

Write the date before the words. Then "this Thursday" or "tomorrow" is only used where it is true on that day.

2

Teach more than you invite

About 40% of the posts invite. The teaching posts are what make the invites worth reading.

3

Invite about every 3 days in the last two weeks

The first invite goes out about two weeks before. Every invite names the date, time and time zone.

4

No link in the post

The registration link goes in the first comment, tagged so you can see which post brought each sign-up.

5

Rotate the teaching posts

The problem, the method, a worked example, the mistake to avoid. Then start again from a new side.

The prompt that plans it

Plan my countdown posts
YOUR TASK
Plan my posts for the 4 weeks before my live session, as a countdown.

THE FACTS TO USE
Session: [FILL: title, date, time and time zone]
Who it is for and what they leave with: [FILL]
My topics: [FILL: the problem, the method, a worked example, the common mistake]

RULES
- 12 posts over 4 weeks, at most one a day.
- About 40% invite people to the session (5 of 12). The rest teach one useful thing.
- Invites go out about every 3 days in the last two weeks.
- Date every post first. Any "this Thursday" or "tomorrow" must be true on the day the post goes out.
- No link in the post itself. The registration link goes in the first comment.

WHAT TO RETURN
A table: date, teach or invite, the topic, the first line, the first comment.

CHECK BEFORE YOU ANSWER
Count the invites. Check every date word against its post date.

Before each post goes out

  • The date words in the post are true on the day it goes out.
  • An invite post names the date, the time and the time zone.
  • The link is in the first comment, not the post.
  • No more than one post that day.
