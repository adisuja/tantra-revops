The 13-Day Cold Email Thread

Five subject lines read as five cold emails from a stranger. One subject line reads as one conversation that gets shorter each time. This is that thread.

The thread: 5 emails, 13 days, 1 subject line

The rules

1

One subject line, on email 1 only

Short and plain, like a note from a colleague. No hype, no number you can't back.

2

One link, never two

Two links is a decision, and a decision gets postponed.

3

One new reason per email

Each reply adds something. None repeats the first email.

4

Every email ends with a way out

One line that makes opting out easy.

5

Only the merge fields your tool supports

{first_name} and {company}, spelled exactly. A misspelled field goes out as raw text.

Before the full send

Send to a small batch first and watch the bounces. If they climb above a few percent, stop and clean the list.

Two versions of every email

Same offer, same link, same days. Only the angle changes, so you find out which one your buyers answer.

DayVersion A: outcomeVersion B: objection
0Open on the outcome your buyer wants. Then the event or resource, and the link.Open on the objection, in your buyer's own words. Then the same event or resource, and the link.
3A proof point that backs the outcome.A proof point that answers the objection.
6The one idea they will learn, tied to the outcome.The one idea they will learn, tied to the objection.
9The date again, with the outcome in one line.The date again, with the objection answered in one line.

The prompt that fills all five

Write my cold email thread
Write a 5-step cold email thread inviting [FILL: buyer role] to [FILL: my event line, resource or offer].
Two versions with the same offer: A leads with the outcome; B leads with this objection in my buyer's words: "[FILL: objection]".
Step 1 on day 0 has a subject line (under 6 words, plain, no hype). Steps 2 to 5 (days 3, 6, 9, 13) are replies in the same thread: under 80 words each, one new reason each, one link only.
Merge fields allowed: {first_name}, {company}. Last line of every step: a one-line opt out.
Use my Funnel Brief. No invented numbers, names or results. If a step needs proof and I have none, write it without proof.

Done when

Both versions are loaded in your tool, a test send to yourself shows every merge field filled, and the first small batch has gone out.

The mistake to avoid

Changing the offer between A and B. Test one thing at a time: the angle.
