The 1-in-5 Post and DM Flow

Posts that only teach never start a conversation. Posts that always ask wear people out. Teach four times, invite a reply once, then follow up twice and stop.

The post mix

PostTypeWhat it does
1TeachOne problem your buyer has, in their words
2TeachOne step of how you solve it
3TeachA worked example
4TeachA common mistake and the fix
5InviteTeach one thing, then ask a question people can answer in one line

The rules

1

About one post in five invites

The other four earn the right to ask.

2

Reply to every comment in the first hour

By hand, never with a tool or a pod.

3

Two follow-ups, then stop

Day 3 and day 10. No reply after that is an answer.

The prompt that writes it

Write my invite post and DM flow
YOUR TASK
Write my reply-inviting post and the DM flow that follows it.

THE FACTS TO USE
My buyer and their problem: [FILL]
The one useful thing I can send: [FILL: a resource, an example or a short answer]

RULES
- The post teaches first, then asks a question people can answer in one line.
- DM 1 answers their reply and asks one question. No pitch.
- Day 3: one short follow-up that adds one useful thing.
- Day 10: the last follow-up. Then stop.
- Plain words, no hype, no fake urgency.

WHAT TO RETURN
The post, then DM 1, the day 3 message and the day 10 message.

CHECK BEFORE YOU ANSWER
Each DM is under 60 words and asks at most one question.
