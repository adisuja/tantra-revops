The 8-Part Prompt Skeleton

Every prompt in our funnel library has the same eight parts, in the same order. You bring the facts. The skeleton brings the structure.

The blank skeleton, with a note on each part

Copy this, then fill each part
YOUR TASK
[The one element you want back. One, not five.]

WHERE THIS SITS IN MY FUNNEL
[The stage, what the reader saw just before, what they do next.]

THE FACTS TO USE
[Your offer, buyer, proof and links. Anything missing: write [FILL].]

THE TEMPLATE
[The shape of the answer, block by block, with lengths.]

RULES
[Length, tone, what to leave out. No invented numbers.]

REFERENCE EXAMPLE
[Paste a real element to mirror. Structure only, never its claims.]

WHAT TO RETURN
[Version A leads with the outcome. Version B answers the objection.]

CHECK BEFORE YOU ANSWER
[The checks the AI runs on its own draft before you see it.]

Why the order matters

The task and the stage come first, so the AI knows what it is writing and for whom. The facts come before the template, so nothing gets made up. The check comes last, so the draft is tested before you read it.

Worked example: a registration page headline

The skeleton, filled in
YOUR TASK
Write the headline and subhead for my registration page.

WHERE THIS SITS IN MY FUNNEL
Stage 2, Landing. The reader just clicked a calendar invite. The next thing they do is fill in the form.

THE FACTS TO USE
Buyer: finance leads at [FILL: company size].
Outcome: close the month in days, not weeks.
Event line: [FILL: title, date, time and time zone].
Proof: [FILL: number, who, when].

THE TEMPLATE
Headline: the promise, under 12 words.
Subhead: the mechanism in one line.

RULES
No hype words. No invented numbers. A missing fact stays [FILL].

REFERENCE EXAMPLE
[PASTE: a headline and subhead from a page that worked]

WHAT TO RETURN
Version A leads with the outcome.
Version B answers this objection: "[FILL: objection, word for word]".

CHECK BEFORE YOU ANSWER
Is every claim in my facts? Is the headline under 12 words? Is there one promise, not two?
