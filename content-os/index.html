The Content OS

The system behind our team's LinkedIn posts. It plans the ideas, drafts the copy and builds the design. A person picks the ideas and approves every post before it goes out.

The weekly loop

StepWhat happensWho
1 ContextOne file holds what you sell, who buys, your proof with sources and the words you never useYou, once
2 IdeasIdeas are pitched, each matched to a post format that already works for othersAI pitches, you pick
3 CopyEach picked idea is written to its format with a hook inside 140 characters, every fact from your context fileAI drafts
4 DesignThe image or carousel is built to match its formatAI builds
5 ReviewEvery post is checked on a phone-sized previewYou approve
6 PublishApproved posts are scheduledYou, or your scheduling tool

The rules every post passes

  • No claim without a source in your context file.
  • The hook sits inside the first 140 characters.
  • No link in the post; the link goes in the first comment.
  • 0 to 3 hashtags.
  • At most one post a day, 2 to 5 a week.

The prompt that pitches your ideas

Pitch 10 post ideas from my context
YOUR TASK
Pitch 10 LinkedIn post ideas for me from my context file.

THE FACTS TO USE
My context file: [PASTE: what I sell, who buys, my proof with sources, my event, words I never use]

RULES
- Each idea teaches one thing my buyer can use, from my own method.
- Each idea has a hook (the first two lines, under 140 characters), the format, and the one fact from my file it uses.
- No number, client or quote that is not in my file.
- About 4 in 10 ideas invite people to my event; the rest teach.

WHAT TO RETURN
A table of 10 ideas. I pick before anything is written.

CHECK BEFORE YOU ANSWER
Every fact in the table appears in my context file.
