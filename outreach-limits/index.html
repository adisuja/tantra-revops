LinkedIn: 3 pacing limits

1

15 to 20 connection requests a day, per profile

Start there. Raise it slowly, and only while acceptance is healthy and LinkedIn shows no warning.

2

14 days, then withdraw the request

A request still open after two weeks comes back off.

3

1 automation tool per profile

Two tools on one profile is the fastest way to lose it.

What goes inside the limit

The LinkedIn note

One specific detail about them, one line on the problem you help with, one low-pressure question. No pitch, no link.

The subject line

Short and plain, like a note from a colleague. No hype, no number you can't back.

The SMS and the WhatsApp message

A plain statement of fact, and one link. Only to people who ticked the box for it.

Every channel

One link per message. One prospect on one sender's list only.
